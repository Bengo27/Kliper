## CS2 (search "cs2 streamer clips")

- Webcam on top, often about **half** the screen; gameplay below.
- Captions: **1–3 words, ALL CAPS**, white with black outline, on the webcam line ("ONE CT", "I CAN HAVE").
- Name tag low on the gameplay. CS2 top bar often kept.
- Top: dorozea 218K, "THE ART OF" 187K, Loba trolling 93K, "next donk" 69K.

## Call of Duty: Black Ops 7

- **xQc 331K:** split, **white rounded text box with black text** (TikTok sticker look) on the line, karaoke
  subtitles with one yellow word, big channel tag (KICK.COM/XQC) on the gameplay.

## Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake (the new Zelda, Nov 2026)

- Top two (152K, 236K): **whole 16:9 stream on black**, short line of text above. Cinematic, story moments.
- Skip2MyLu 77K: webcam top with rounded corners, **white banner with black ALL-CAPS text** on the line,
  YouTube + Twitch logos and name on the gameplay.
- moistcr1tikal 11K: split, **one word at a time** captions on the line.
- A **chat pop-up**: coloured box "name: message" over the webcam when the streamer reacts to chat.
